### Typography:
- **Title (शुभ विवाह):** 80-100px, Noto Serif Devanagari, Gold (#C8A45D)
- **Couple Names:** 48-60px, Playfair Display, Gold Light (#E8D6A5)
- **Date & Location:** 36-40px, Playfair Display, Ivory (#FFF8ED)
- **Company Name:** 28-32px, Inter, Ivory/70%

### Decorative Elements:
- Golden border/frame
- Floral corner decorations
- Subtle mandala pattern in background

## 🛠️ Quick Creation Methods

### Method 1: Canva (Recommended)
1. Go to [canva.com](https://canva.com)
2. Create Custom Design → 1200 x 630 px
3. Search for "Indian Wedding Invitation" templates
4. Customize with your text:
   - शुभ विवाह
   - Aayu. Ekta & Vi. Vikas
   - Aayu. Priyanka & Vi. Bijendra
   - December 2026
   - Gunti, Behror, Rajasthan
5. Download as JPG
6. Rename to `wedding-preview.jpg`

### Method 2: Use Invitation Card
1. Open invitation card image in any image editor
2. Resize canvas to 1200 x 630 px (crop from center)
3. Add white text overlay if needed for readability
4. Export as `wedding-preview.jpg`

### Method 3: Screenshot Website
1. Open your deployed website
2. Use browser to take full-page screenshot
3. Crop the hero section (where शुभ विवाह appears)
4. Resize to exactly 1200 x 630 px
5. Save as `wedding-preview.jpg`

## 📱 Testing Your Preview

After creating and uploading your image:

1. **Deploy to Vercel**
2. **Test with Facebook Debugger:**
   - https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/
   - Enter your URL
   - Click "Scrape Again"
   
3. **Share on WhatsApp:**
   - Send link to yourself
   - Preview should appear within 5 seconds

4. **If preview doesn't show:**
   - Check file is at `/public/wedding-preview.jpg`
   - Verify file size is under 1MB
   - Use Facebook debugger to clear cache
   - Wait 24 hours for WhatsApp to refresh

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Use High Contrast:** Make text stand out from background
2. **Keep It Simple:** Don't overcrowd the design
3. **Test on Mobile:** Preview looks different on phone
4. **Update Domain:** Remember to replace `your-vercel-domain.vercel.app` with actual URL
5. **Avoid Small Text:** People view on small screens
6. **Use Wedding Colors:** Maroon & gold theme throughout
